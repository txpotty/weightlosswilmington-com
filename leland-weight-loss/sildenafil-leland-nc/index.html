    How Sildenafil Works in Leland

    Sildenafil blocks PDE5. PDE5 breaks down cGMP, and cGMP is what relaxes the blood vessels in the penis, so with the enzyme blocked, blood flow during arousal improves. It does nothing without sexual stimulation. The pill does not create desire or an erection out of nowhere. What it does is make an erection easier to reach and to hold.

    Who Sildenafil Is For

    The usual candidate is an adult man with erectile dysfunction who has sex now and then and would rather take a pill only when needed. It works whether the cause is blood flow, nerves, stress or some mix. Men with diabetes or high blood pressure often respond well, though those conditions need their own care too. Some men end up better suited to tadalafil. That is usually men who want a longer window.

    Sildenafil Dosing and Timing in Leland

    Take it by mouth 30 to 60 minutes before sex. Common strengths are 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg, and most men start at 50 mg. No more than one dose in any 24 hour period. A heavy or high-fat meal can delay it by an hour or so, which is why many men take it on a light stomach. The effect usually lasts four to five hours.

    What the First Few Weeks Look Like

    Plenty of men notice a difference with the first dose. Others need a few tries to get the timing and the meal situation right. Providers generally suggest trying a dose on four to eight separate occasions before calling it a failure, since nerves and alcohol can throw off the first attempt or two. Mild headache and flushing are common early. Jot down the dose, how long before sex you took it and how it went. That record is what your provider uses to adjust.

    Sildenafil Side Effects in Leland

    Headache, facial flushing, a stuffy nose and indigestion are the most common side effects. A temporary blue tinge or light sensitivity in vision happens to some men, usually at 100 mg. Alcohol and blood pressure pills both make dizziness more likely, especially at 100 mg and especially when standing up fast. An erection longer than four hours, or any sudden loss of vision or hearing, is an emergency room situation, not a message to your provider. Rare, but worth knowing.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule sildenafil out. Nitroglycerin, isosorbide and poppers are all nitrates, and pairing any of them with sildenafil can crash blood pressure. Riociguat is also off the table. Questions cover recent heart attack or stroke, chest pain that comes on at rest and serious liver or kidney problems. One more question matters: can you climb two flights of stairs without chest pain?

    Sildenafil Compared to Other ED Options in Leland

    Sildenafil kicks in within an hour and fades in about four to five hours. Tadalafil covers up to 36 hours, or can be taken every day at a low dose. Chewables, troches and rapid dissolve tablets are compounded formats, and compounded products are not FDA-approved. Generic sildenafil is FDA-approved and is chemically the same as Viagra, so it is often where providers start for men who want a short, predictable window. Short window, long track record.
