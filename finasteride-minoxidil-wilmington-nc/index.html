    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Wilmington, NC

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Wilmington

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. Lower DHT protects follicles from the slow miniaturization behind pattern loss. Minoxidil adds something different. It extends the growth phase of each hair and supports thicker strands, likely by improving blood flow and signaling at the follicle.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. Early treatment keeps the most hair, since long-bald spots seldom respond. Some women past menopause use it off-label. Women who could become pregnant cannot.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Wilmington

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Minoxidil comes as a twice-daily topical or a low-dose off-label tablet. One spray daily also works. A compounded topical that combines finasteride and minoxidil goes on the scalp once a day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. The early shed is common and usually temporary. By months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening at the crown. Long-term trials showed most men held their hair for years. Full results show near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Wilmington

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. Most of these fade after stopping, and mood changes need prompt reporting. Scalp dryness is the usual topical issue. Oral minoxidil can cause body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat. Topical finasteride may lower whole-body exposure.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. Expect questions about your medication list and family plans, because both can shape the choice. Finasteride lowers PSA readings. Women who could become pregnant should not handle crushed or broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Wilmington

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. Dutasteride + minoxidil is a stronger, off-label option, usually considered after a year on finasteride without enough change in the photos. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is topical-only. Minoxidil by itself leaves the hormonal cause untreated.
